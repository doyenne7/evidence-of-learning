The 3-Year Measurement Roadmap

Each year builds on the prior, with increasing rigor and validity.

Year 1

Collecting Early Evidence of Learning

Lightweight formative signals woven into every lesson — automatic and low-friction for students and teachers.

  • 🔵 Engagement signals — lesson completion, time on task, activity attempts
  • 🟡 Confidence checks — pre/post emoji self-ratings before and after lessons
  • 🟣 Embedded concept checks — 1–2 quick questions mapped to each lesson's objectives
  • 🟢 Micro-reflections — short text prompts ("What surprised you?", "What was hardest?")
  • 🟠 Lesson sentiment — end-of-lesson mood ("Too hard / Just right / Too easy")

Year 2

Refining Learning Measurement

Year 1 signals are aggregated into teacher dashboards; formative assessment gains stronger scaffolding and feedback loops.

  • 📊 Teacher dashboards — concept mastery heatmaps, class-level trend lines
  • 📈 Learning progression tracking — individual student growth over 5 lessons
  • 🔍 Misconception detection — flagging common wrong-answer patterns
  • 🏫 Stronger formative items — richer scenario-based questions per lesson
  • 🤝 Facilitation insights — "Class needs more practice on Lesson 2 bias concept"
📊 View Teacher Dashboard →
↓
Year 3

Validated Learning Outcomes Framework

Two years of data inform a robust, evidence-backed assessment framework aligned to AI4K12 and CSTA standards.

  • ✅ Validated learning outcomes — pre/post assessments with reliability data
  • 📐 Rubric-based evaluation — 4-level progression rubrics per learning objective
  • 🏛️ Standards alignment — mapped to AI4K12 Big Ideas (BI-3, BI-5, BI-2)
  • 📋 School-system reporting — shareable evidence summaries for districts
  • 🔬 Continuous validity — ongoing refinement based on multi-year evidence
