# Evidence of Learning — 3-Year Measurement Roadmap Prototype

> **Grant Proposal Prototype** for Code.org's mobile-first K–5 AI curriculum  
> *"How AI Makes Decisions"* — 5-lesson module, upper elementary

## Overview

This prototype demonstrates how we can collect **early evidence of learning from Year 1** while iteratively building toward a validated outcomes framework by Year 3. It directly addresses the grant requirement for assessment measures starting in Year 1, without waiting for the full learning-outcomes infrastructure of Year 3.

### Our Position
> *"We are not waiting until Year 3 to think about assessment. We are starting with lightweight formative learning signals from Year 1, so we can continuously improve the experience and iteratively build toward learning-outcomes measurement appropriate for school systems."*

---

## The 3-Year Roadmap

| Year | Focus | Key Measures |
|------|-------|--------------|
| **Year 1** | Collecting Early Evidence | Engagement signals, confidence checks, concept checks (2 per lesson), micro-reflections, lesson sentiment |
| **Year 2** | Refining Measurement | Teacher dashboards, misconception detection, learning progression, facilitation insights |
| **Year 3** | Validated Outcomes | Pre/post assessments (α=0.81), rubric scoring, district-level reporting, school-system scalability |

## Course: How AI Makes Decisions

5 lessons aligned to AI4K12 National Guidelines and CSTA K-12 Standards:

1. **Making Predictions** — Sorting by features, supervised/reinforcement learning
2. **Training AI** — Training data impact, AI for Oceans, bias in ML
3. **Using AI Bot** — Feature vectors, Dance Party AI, human-AI collaboration
4. **AI Tools in School** — Student data analysis, ethics of AI decisions
5. **Share Your Voice** — Opinion artifacts, module reflection

## Year 1 Assessment Signal Types

- 🔵 **Behavioral** — Auto-captured from activity interactions
- 🟣 **Cognitive** — Embedded concept checks (1–2 questions per lesson)
- 🟡 **Affective** — Confidence checks, lesson sentiment
- ✍️ **Reflective** — Micro-reflection prompts, open text
