⚠️ Common Gaps & Misconceptions

Auto-detected from wrong-answer patterns across all concept checks.

🔴 AI Bias — Lesson 2
Q: "What happens when training data is biased?"
39% wrong
❌ Most common wrong answer: "Different opinions in training data don't affect AI's accuracy"
❌ Second most common: "Biased data only affects AI for 1 day then it corrects itself"
💡 Suggested Intervention
Re-teach the "Attendance Bot" scenario from Lesson 2 Wrap Up. Ask: "What if only tall students were marked Present?" Connect bias to lasting, real-world consequences.
🟡 Feature Vectors — Lesson 3
Q: "How does AI use feature vectors to make decisions?"
27% wrong
❌ Most common wrong answer: "AI randomly picks from available options"
💡 Suggested Intervention
Re-run the food feature vector activity as a whole-class demo. Emphasize the axes and show how proximity in the grid determines AI's choice.
🟡 Human Oversight — Lessons 2 & 3
Q: "Should AI make final decisions without humans?"
23% wrong
❌ Common wrong answer: "AI always makes better decisions than humans, so it should work alone"
💡 Suggested Intervention
Use the Lesson 4 Wrap Up scenario (AI Activity Picker only recommending beach activities) to show failure cases that require human correction.
