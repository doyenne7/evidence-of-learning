# Demo backups: AI for Oceans, Lesson 1 Level 4

Backup clips for live demos of the [AI for Oceans tutorial, level 4](https://studio.code.org/courses/oceans/units/1/lessons/1/levels/4), recorded with Loom on 2026-08-22.

| File | What it shows | Length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `level4_training_model.gif` / `.mp4` | Training the AI: labeling images as belonging in the water or not | ~39s |
| `level4_testing_model.gif` / `.mp4` | Testing the trained model: A.I. bot sorting ocean objects based on the training | ~20s |

The GIFs are cropped to remove the browser chrome, sized 900px wide at 12fps, and loop continuously — ready to drop into a slide deck (in Google Slides: **Insert → Image**, and they autoplay). The MP4s are the original recordings for when a pausable video is preferable.
